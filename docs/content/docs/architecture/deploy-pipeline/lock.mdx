Each service has one <Term id="service-lock" />. A <Term id="deploy" /> takes it at the start of the state `deploying` and keeps it until the deploy ends.

## Why it exists

Many parts of the engine change containers: a deploy, the <Term id="reconciler" />, a scale, a suspend. With the lock, they do not touch the containers of a deploy in progress.

The reconciler never waits for the lock. It [skips a busy service](/docs/architecture/reconciler/services) until its next pass. Thus the reconciler never works against a deploy.
