## Who publishes a port

| Container | Published port |
|---|---|
| Web services, private services, static sites | The container port, on `127.0.0.1:<random port>` |
| Background workers, cron jobs, job runs | None |

## The container port

At each deploy, Ferry chooses the container port and gives it to the app in `PORT`. The app must listen on that port on `0.0.0.0`. An app that listens on `127.0.0.1` in its container fails the health check. See [The port of your app](/docs/concepts/deploys/ports).

To know why the address is `127.0.0.1`, see [Why 127.0.0.1](/docs/architecture/networking-model/why-localhost).
