Ferry is for one team that runs its own server. A server has one <Term id="admin-account" />. The person who signs in with it, or who has one of its <Term id="api-token">API tokens</Term>, controls the full server.

## What the model protects

- The account, the tokens and the secrets stay on the server.
- Only the <Term id="proxy" /> is on the network.
- One runaway app cannot stop the server.
- Two Ferry servers cannot damage the containers of each other.

Ferry has no other users, no roles and no permissions per service. Read the [known limitations](/docs/architecture/security/limitations) before you put a server on the internet.

## Connect from your machine

On a production server, keep the <Term id="api" /> on <Term id="localhost" />. Reach it through an <Term id="ssh-tunnel" />.

```bash title="Terminal"
ssh -L 7878:127.0.0.1:7878 you@your-server
ferry login --server http://127.0.0.1:7878
```
