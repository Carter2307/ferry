## What your app must do

Listen on the port in `PORT`, on the address `0.0.0.0`.

```js title="server.js"
const port = process.env.PORT;
app.listen(port, '0.0.0.0');
```

<Callout type="warning">
An app that listens on `127.0.0.1` in its container gets no traffic.
</Callout>

