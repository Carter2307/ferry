Ferry cannot resolve a <Term id="reference" /> in these cases:

- the kind, the name or the property is unknown
- the service has no public URL

Then the <Term id="deploy" /> or the <Term id="restart" /> fails with a clear error. The live deploy continues to answer.

## What the CLI checks

- The <Term id="cli" /> checks the syntax before it saves.
- The CLI gives a warning for a name that does not exist yet.

The [reference syntax page](/docs/reference/env-references) lists the errors.

## Delete a datastore or a service that others use

Ferry refuses to delete a <Term id="datastore" /> or a service that other services reference. The error lists who uses it.

`--force` deletes it anyway. Then the services that reference it fail at their next deploy or restart.
