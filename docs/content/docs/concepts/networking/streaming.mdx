## What goes through the proxy

| Traffic | Through the proxy |
|---|---|
| <Term id="websocket">WebSockets</Term> and other HTTP/1.1 upgrades | Yes. The proxy makes a tunnel to your app. |
| `h2c` upgrades | No. The proxy sends those requests as plain HTTP/1.1. |
| <Term id="sse" /> and streamed answers | Yes. The timeouts of the proxy never cut an answer in progress. |
| <Term id="http2" /> from clients | Yes: with <Term id="tls" />, and in cleartext with prior knowledge. |
| <Term id="grpc" /> and other protocols that need HTTP/2 end to end | No. |

## Use gRPC

The <Term id="proxy" /> always uses HTTP/1.1 with your app. Thus gRPC does not work through the public proxy.

Call a gRPC service on the [private network](/docs/concepts/networking/private-network).
