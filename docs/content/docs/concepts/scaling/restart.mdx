A <Term id="restart" /> is a new <Term id="deploy" /> with the <Term id="trigger" /> `restart`. It uses the image of the live deploy again. It builds nothing.

## What a restart applies

A restart brings your saved changes to a service that runs:

- variables
- links to <Term id="env-group">env groups</Term>
- settings
- [resource limits](/docs/concepts/resource-limits)

`ferry env set` and `ferry env-group set` restart the service for you.

## The rule

The service must have a <Term id="live-deploy" />.

[Restart a service](/docs/concepts/deploys/restarts) shows the steps of a restart. [Deploys](/docs/concepts/deploys) explains rollbacks, cancels and statuses.
