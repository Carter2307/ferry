title: Delete a service
description: A delete removes the service and all that belongs to it. You cannot undo it.
---

```bash title="Terminal"
ferry delete api
```

## What Ferry removes

- The containers of the service.
- The images that Ferry built for it.
- Its <Term id="disk" />.
- Its <Term id="git-cache" /> and its uploads.
- Its deploy logs and job logs.
- Its settings and variables.

<Callout type="warning" title="You cannot undo a delete">
The data on the disk of the service goes too.
</Callout>

## If other services use it

Other services can have a <Term id="reference" /> to the service in their environment: `${{service.api.…}}`. Then Ferry refuses the delete.

```bash title="Terminal"
ferry delete api --force
```

After a delete with `--force`, remove the reference from the other services. Until then, their deploys and restarts fail.
