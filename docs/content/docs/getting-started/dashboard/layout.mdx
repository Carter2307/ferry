## The top bar

| Side | What it has |
|---|---|
| Left | The <Term id="breadcrumb" />: the server, with its default domain and its version. On a service: the switcher, the type and the state. |
| Right | **Connect**, **Docs**, the search field, the theme menu, help, **Sign out** and the account menu. |

- The theme menu has three values: light, dark or system.
- The account menu shows the email of your account. It opens **Server → Account**.
- [**Connect**](/docs/getting-started/dashboard/connect) and [the search field](/docs/getting-started/dashboard/command-palette) have their own pages.

## The icon rail

| Icon | Page |
|---|---|
| **Services** | [The home page](/docs/getting-started/dashboard/services) |
| **Datastores** | [Datastores](/docs/getting-started/dashboard/datastores) |
| **Env groups** | [Env groups](/docs/getting-started/dashboard/env-groups) |
| **Blueprints** | [Blueprints](/docs/getting-started/dashboard/blueprints) |
| **Server** | [Server](/docs/getting-started/dashboard/server) |

The button at the bottom of the <Term id="icon-rail" /> shows or hides the labels. On a phone, the rail becomes a menu button in the top bar.
