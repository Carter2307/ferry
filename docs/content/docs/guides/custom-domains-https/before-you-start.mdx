| You need | How |
|---|---|
| <Term id="https" /> on the server | <Term id="ferryd" /> runs with `--https-addr 0.0.0.0:443` and `--acme-email`. |
| <Term id="port">Port</Term> 80 open to the internet | The <Term id="proxy" /> of the server listens on it: `--proxy-addr 0.0.0.0:80`. |
| A <Term id="web-service" /> or a <Term id="static-site" /> | <Term id="private-service">Private services</Term>, <Term id="worker">workers</Term> and <Term id="cron-job">cron jobs</Term> cannot have domains. |

Without the two HTTPS options, the domain works over HTTP only. See [Production setup](/docs/guides/production).

## Why port 80

<Term id="lets-encrypt" /> connects to port 80 to validate the domain. The port of your site does not change this.

It asks for `http://shop.example.com/.well-known/acme-challenge/…`. The proxy answers with the key authorization.
