---
title: The health check times out
description: Ferry cannot connect to the port of the new instance. The app listens on the wrong address or port.
---

## What you see

```text title="Deploy log" noCopy
==> Deploy failed: health check timed out after 120s: nothing accepts connections on container port 10000 (published as 127.0.0.1:…) yet: does the app listen on $PORT (10000) on 0.0.0.0? An app listening on 127.0.0.1 (localhost) inside its container can't be reached
```

The <Term id="health-check" /> connects to the <Term id="port" /> of the new <Term id="instance" />. Nothing answers on that port.

The deploy gets the status `deploy_failed`. The previous deploy continues to answer.

## How to repair it

Make the app listen on `0.0.0.0` and on the port in `$PORT`. As an alternative, give the port of your app to Ferry:

```bash title="Terminal"
ferry update NAME --port N
```

See [The port of your app](/docs/concepts/deploys/ports) and [Environment](/docs/concepts/environment).

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="The app needs more time to start">
    Give a longer timeout to the full server: `ferryd --health-check-timeout`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You set PORT to another value">
    If you set `PORT` to a value that is not the port of the service, the message is different:

    `the app gets PORT=… from its environment but Ferry expects it to listen on port …`
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
